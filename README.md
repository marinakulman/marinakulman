## Marina Kulman

Data & BI Analyst in Dublin. Over a decade in commercial analytics across FMCG -
category management, BI reporting and stakeholder-facing analysis at Danone, GSK
and Carlsberg.

I work on the part of analytics that changes a decision: defining the metric
correctly, checking it against the data, and saying plainly what it does and
does not support.

### Projects

**[Shannon Energy - Tableau](https://github.com/marinakulman/shannon-energy-tableau)**
Channel economics and retention for an energy retailer. The cheapest acquisition
channel turned out to be the most expensive to keep a customer from. Built on a
reproducible Python pipeline that asserts 14 control values and fails loudly if
any figure drifts.
*Interactive dashboard · Tableau Public*

**[Shannon Energy - Power BI](https://github.com/marinakulman/shannon-energy-powerbi)**
The same business problem, built independently in Power BI: Power Query cleaning,
29 DAX measures, row-level security. Both projects arrive at the same figures -
reproducing a result by a second route is how the method was checked.

**[Portfolio site](https://marinakulman.github.io/Portfolio/)**
All three projects, including a dairy category review built to Nielsen retail
audit and GfK panel structure.
